Southern Ocean metrics with CMIP6
=================================

Overview
--------

``recipe_russell18jgr_cmip6.yml`` applies the diagnostics described in
:doc:`recipe_russell18jgr` to CMIP6 historical simulations for 1986--2005.
The original ``recipe_russell18jgr.yml`` retains its CMIP5 input selection.
Both recipes use the same Python diagnostic scripts and options.

The CMIP6 recipe includes ACCESS-ESM1-5 and three representatives of model
families in the CMIP5 recipe.
These are different model generations, not interchangeable simulations.
All selected simulations use ``r1i1p1f1``.

.. list-table:: Model selection
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 25 50

   * - CMIP6 model
     - CMIP5 family comparison
     - Diagnostic coverage
   * - ACCESS-ESM1-5
     - Additional ACCESS model
     - All groups except Figure 8 (pH).
   * - CanESM5
     - CanESM2
     - All except Figures 6a, 6b and 8; Figure 4 shows velocity only.
   * - GFDL-ESM4
     - GFDL-ESM2G / GFDL-ESM2M
     - All except Figures 4, 6a and 6b.
   * - IPSL-CM6A-LR
     - IPSL-CM5A / IPSL-CM5B
     - All except Figures 6a and 6b; Figure 4 shows velocity only.

The selection reflects the published fields available in the Gadi archives
used for testing.
It is a representative subset, not a complete mapping of every CMIP5 model.
ACCESS-ESM1-5 supplies fixed ``Ofx volcello`` for Figures 4 and 6.
The selected CanESM5 and IPSL-CM6A-LR datasets lack ``volcello``, so their
Figure 4 panels contain velocity without an integrated transport estimate.
The selected GFDL-ESM4 dataset lacks ``uo`` and ``vo``.
Historical ``ph`` was not found for ACCESS-ESM1-5 or CanESM5, so Figure 8
uses GFDL-ESM4 and IPSL-CM6A-LR.

Input conventions
-----------------

CMIP6 sea ice concentration is ``SImon siconc`` and fixed ocean fields use
the ``Ofx`` table.
Ocean fields retain their published land masks.
Atmospheric ``tauu`` is land masked by the preprocessor.

Most inputs use the ``gn`` grid.
For GFDL-ESM4, atmospheric wind stress uses ``gr1`` and carbon flux, pH and
heat flux use ``gr`` with matching ``gr areacello`` where needed.
Its sea ice and sea ice cell areas use ``gn``.
IPSL-CM6A-LR atmospheric wind stress uses ``gr``.
Grid selections are explicit in the recipe; no additional regridding is
introduced.

Interpretation
--------------

The recipe retains the legacy methods to support comparison with CMIP5.
Figure 1 uses atmospheric ``tauu`` while Figures 2 and 9 use ocean surface
``tauuo``; these are different stress fields.
Figure 5 uses September and March, following the existing diagnostic;
the published paper uses February for the summer extent.

Figures 4 and 6 estimate transport from climatological fields using cell
volumes to approximate section areas.
Figure 6 does not compute the mean of monthly density-layer transports or
resolve velocity--temperature covariance, and uses fixed density and heat
capacity for heat transport.
The results should not be interpreted as validated model-native fluxes.
Figure 9 regressions describe the selected CMIP6 models and do not reproduce
the paper's ensemble or reported statistical significance.

The diagnostics handle longitude wrapping and overlapping grid columns when
drawing polar maps.
Front extraction orders longitude within each grid row, since a single
ordering is insufficient for some curvilinear grids.
This can correct erroneous front positions on affected grids; the temperature
criteria and transport formulas are unchanged.
